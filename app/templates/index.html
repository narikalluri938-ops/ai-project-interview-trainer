The Real Problem

"I have 3 projects on my resume, but I freeze when the interviewer probes."

Thousands of engineering students put frameworks, databases, and AI models on their resumes without realizing interviewers don't test memory — they test depth, architectural rationale, and edge cases.

Superficial Answers: Saying "I used Flask because it's lightweight" triggers immediate interviewer follow-ups that expose gaps.
Over-Claiming Traps: Listing complex neural networks when you only ran a pre-trained script invites brutal math questions.
No Trade-off Awareness: Unable to explain "Why MySQL over MongoDB?" or "What happens if 10,000 users hit it simultaneously?"
Interview Reality Check
A typical campus placement trap
Interviewer:
"I see you implemented FaceNet and MySQL for attendance. How do you prevent duplicate records if the student stands in front of the camera for 10 seconds?"
Unprepared Candidate:
"Uh... we just inserted into MySQL using Python."
Trainer-Trained Candidate:
"We created a composite unique index on (student_id, date) with an insert debounce check, catching integrity errors at the database layer."
